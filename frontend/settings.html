Shopify order tag rules

When an order carries one of these tags, the Ship page pre-selects the courier service and/or sets the signature requirement sent to the carrier. Packers can still change either before buying. Service names match across providers (e.g. “UPS Ground” matches “UPS® Ground”).

Profit check

Before a label is bought, the order's profit (items after discounts + shipping paid − product costs − the label) is checked against these minimums. If either fails, the purchase is blocked until the bypass password is entered; every override is recorded in the audit log. Leave both blank to turn the check off. Costs come from Shopify's inventory unit cost and BackOffice's invoice line cost — an item without a cost counts at 90% of its selling price (an assumed 10% margin, so one uncosted product does not block an order).

Rule presets

Combine saved rules with AND / OR into one filter. Each preset is a saved-view tab on the Pick lists page: while one is on, it decides which orders go on the pick list and the rules pause; the other filters still narrow on top.
