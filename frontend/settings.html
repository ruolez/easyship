How profit is worked out

Profit = items after discounts + shipping the customer paid − product costs − the label. Costs come from Shopify’s inventory unit cost and BackOffice’s invoice line cost. An item without a cost counts at 90% of its selling price (an assumed 10% margin), so one uncosted product does not block an order. Every override is recorded in the audit log.

Rule presets

Combine saved rules with AND / OR into one filter. Each preset is a saved-view tab on the Pick lists page: while one is on, it decides which orders go on the pick list and the rules pause; the other filters still narrow on top.
